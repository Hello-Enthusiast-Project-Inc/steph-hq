Rolling Scramble — Arrive & Play
How it works
1
Sign in when you arrive and form a team of 2 players. Pair up when you arrive.
2
Pick a team name. Tank top themed names encouraged. 👕
3
Play all 6 holes. Everyone hits each shot — play from the best ball. Count total team strokes.
4
New teams start at the next open hole and work their way around.
Prizes
🥇 Lowest team score — Champion
🎯 Hole-in-one — Victory prize
Keep it casual · Have fun! 🎉
