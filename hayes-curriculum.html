Curriculum Units

👋 Unit 1: Hello World (6–8 months) — Senses & Face-to-Face Connection
🖐️ Unit 2: The Senses (7–9 months) — Sensory Exploration & Cause-and-Effect
🐻 Unit 3: Animals (9–11 months) — Animal Sounds & Early Communication
🌈 Unit 4: Colours (11–13 months) — Colour Recognition & Sorting
❤️ Unit 5: My Body (13–15 months) — Body Awareness & Movement
🔷 Unit 6: Shapes & Sizes (15–18 months) — Shapes, Sorting & Pretend Play

How to Use This Curriculum:

Each unit contains 8 weeks of activities. Each week has 5 days (Monday–Friday) with:

  • A developmental milestone focus
  • A read-aloud (book + instructions)
  • A motor or sensory activity
  • A song or poem
  • Woven ideas (things to do throughout the day)

Guiding Principles:

  • All activities are designed for solo parent + baby
  • Books and activities are based on research (Zero to Three, CDC, AAP)
  • Activities are brief (10–15 min) and woven into daily routines
  • No visual tracking exercises (family preference)
  • Progress is natural — some weeks will feel easier than others

A Note from Lumen:

Hayes is 6 months old now. Over the next 12 months, he'll grow, learn, and amaze you every single day. This curriculum is a guide, not a prescription. Some days he'll be into sensory play, other days he'll just want to cuddle and watch you work. Trust your instincts, follow his lead, and celebrate the small victories.
