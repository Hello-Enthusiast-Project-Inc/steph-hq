Morning Glory
Muffins

Makes 12  ·  31 minutes  ·  Wholesome & naturally sweet

🧁🌿🥕

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mashed banana (2 large bananas)
  • 3 large eggs, at room temperature
  • 3 tbsp refined coconut oil, melted
  • ¼ cup unsweetened almond milk
  • ⅓ cup (85g) creamy almond butter
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cup (43g) coconut flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • ¾ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ cup freshly shredded carrot
  • ½ cup chopped toasted pecans
  • ⅓ cup golden raisins

Swaps & Notes

Nut-free: swap almond butter for sunflower seed butter or tahini, use nut-free milk, skip the pecans.

Storage: Airtight at room temp 2 days · fridge up to 1 week · freeze up to 3 months. Microwave 30 sec to thaw.

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 12-cup muffin tin with paper liners or grease with coconut oil.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine mashed banana, eggs, coconut oil, almond milk, almond butter, and vanilla. Mix until fully combined.
  3. Add coconut flour, cinnamon, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Mix well.
  4. Fold in shredded carrot, toasted pecans, and golden raisins.
  5. Divide batter among muffin cups, filling each about two-thirds full.
  6. Bake 16–19 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean and the top springs back when lightly pressed.
  7. Cool in the tin on a wire rack for 10 minutes, then remove and cool completely.

Why You'll Love These

Naturally sweetened with banana, packed with carrot and good fats from almond butter — these taste indulgent but are the kind of thing you feel good eating. Perfect for mornings, snacks, or gifting.
